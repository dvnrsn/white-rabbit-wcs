**The EveryBunny Open!**

We’re switching things up for this month’s event!

With so many amazing dance events happening around AZ over these next couple of weeks, we don’t want to create a situation where dancers feel like they have to choose one event over another or pull people away from events happening in nearby local dance communities.

So, instead of our planned intensive, we’re pivoting to a Jack & Jill competition - THE EVERYBUNNY OPEN! 🎉 It feels like a fun way to bring everyone together, get some extra comp practice in with DCS coming up, and still have a great night of dancing. Check out the event page [here](https://www.facebook.com/share/1FKhnd6m5v/).

And don’t worry! We’re still bringing Gabe to teach a workshop soon! Stay tuned for more details on that!
